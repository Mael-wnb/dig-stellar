== Lot S1 — rate-limit proof (2026-08-17, ~08:20–08:35 UTC) ==
Bursts were run FROM THE VPS over one keepalive connection (a cold-TLS client at
transatlantic RTT physically cannot exceed ~5 r/s — an earlier attempt of 80
sequential curls from the dev machine passed 80/80, which is itself evidence that
a normal remote client never trips the limits).

--- GENERAL zone (rate=10r/s burst=60 nodelay): 100 x GET /health, one connection ---
200 x64, then 429 x36  (trip point 65 = burst 60 + ~4 refilled during the burst)

--- ACTIONS zone (rate=30r/m burst=10 nodelay): 15 x POST /v1/actions/sdex/quote ---
400 x11, then 429 x4   (400 = Nest validation of the empty test body — expected;
                        trip point 12 = burst 10 + 1 refill)

--- MUTATIONS zone (rate=2r/s burst=20 nodelay): 30 x POST /v1/notifications/read-all ---
400 x21, then 429 x9   (trip point 22 = burst 20 + 1 refill; run after a 12 s
                        cooldown so the general zone was full — an earlier run
                        without cooldown tripped at 5 via the still-drained
                        general zone, i.e. the zones compose correctly)

--- GET exemption from the mutations zone ---
30 x GET /v1/notifications/unread-count immediately after draining the mutations
zone: 400 x30 (Nest missing-userId response), zero 429 — reads are keyed out of
the mutations zone by the $request_method map.

--- 429 response is honest JSON with Retry-After + readable cross-origin ---
HTTP/1.1 429 Too Many Requests
Retry-After: 1
Access-Control-Allow-Origin: https://stellar.getdig.ai
Access-Control-Allow-Credentials: true

{"statusCode":429,"error":"Too Many Requests","message":"Rate limit exceeded - please slow down."}

--- NORMAL NAVIGATION stays clean ---
3 rapid simulated page loads (20 parallel requests each across the real dashboard
endpoints, from the dev machine): 60/60 served, zero 429. (Non-200s in the raw
output are application-level: missing userId params / a fabricated slug.)
